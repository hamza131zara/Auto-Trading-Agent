## Traceability

Every requirement maps to a unique ID, design/implementation component or future phase, verifiable acceptance test, expected result, evidence artifact/version, status and owner/risk dependency. Screenshots and documents alone do not prove enforcement; security/trading behavior needs backend/runtime tests.

## Phase 2 exit checklist

- [ ] Product/system boundaries approved.
- [ ] Requirements mapped to verifiable acceptance criteria, including unknown/failure states.
- [ ] Quantitative formulas, units, net-PnL cost treatment and evaluation methodology are unambiguous.
- [ ] Every numeric limit that can permit execution is owner-approved; remaining TBDs are explicitly excluded from executable scope.
- [ ] Backtest/OOS/paper/shadow gates are defined without promising profitability.
- [ ] Portal/runtime separation, license/payment workflow and instance isolation are explicit.
- [ ] Setup docs, guided booking/Meet flow and support boundary have acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Theme, accessibility, responsiveness and data truthfulness requirements are ready for Phase 4.
- [ ] Payment/legal/tax/crypto/provider dependencies remain visibly pending, not mislabeled approved.
- [ ] Threat/custody questions are handed to Phase 3, with roadmap order preserved.

Do not fabricate thresholds, availability, payment/settlement status, provider results, analytics or booking URLs. Do not activate card/crypto payment or mainnet trading from this register.
