# Brand Identity — Auto-Trading-Agent

**Status:** Initial vector identity added for Phase 2 handoff.  
**Purpose:** Establish a consistent professional identity for future Phase 4 screen design and later UI implementation.

## Approved-in-project assets

- [Dark-theme logo lockup](../../assets/brand/auto-trading-agent-logo-dark.svg) — transparent background, light wordmark and gradient symbol for dark surfaces.
- [Light-theme logo lockup](../../assets/brand/auto-trading-agent-logo-light.svg) — transparent background, dark wordmark and gradient symbol for light surfaces.
- [Brand mark / icon](../../assets/brand/auto-trading-agent-mark.svg) — compact standalone signal/market mark for navigation, avatar/favicon exploration and constrained layouts.

The assets are authored as SVG vector files so they can scale without raster blur. Use the light lockup on light backgrounds and the dark lockup on dark backgrounds. Do not place either on a surface with insufficient contrast.

- [3D market-intelligence hero illustration](../../assets/brand/auto-trading-agent-3d-hero.svg) — layered isometric core, orbit rings and a floating chart panel for selective use on hero/product/architecture sections.

## Concept

The mark combines a geometric A-like structure, ascending bars and a directional line. It signals analysis, market movement and automation without implying guaranteed performance or representing any particular token/DEX as endorsed.

Brand tone: professional, analytical, controlled, transparent and self-hosted.

## Visual system direction

- Core neutrals: deep navy/graphite for dark surfaces; soft white/cool neutral for light surfaces.
- Brand accents: restrained blue-to-cyan-to-teal gradient, used primarily for the logo and selected focus/active elements.
- Status colors remain semantic and accessible; never rely on the brand gradient to indicate risk or PnL.
- Use a clean sans-serif wordmark, disciplined spacing, aligned financial numerals and restrained decoration.
- Avoid casino-style effects, excessive glow, rapid animation and unsupported claims such as “safe profits” or “guaranteed trades.”

## Usage rules

1. Preserve the SVG aspect ratio; do not stretch, skew, re-color or add filters to the logo.
2. Keep adequate clear space around the mark; do not overlap text, chart axes or status badges.
3. Do not reduce the full lockup to favicon size; use the compact mark and test its legibility.
4. Always pair the logo with accessible text where branding is the only context/identifier.
5. The logo is a brand asset, not a certification of strategy quality, security, regulatory approval or profitability.

## Phase 4 design acceptance

Review the asset on dark/light navigation, public marketing header, customer portal, runtime dashboard, small mobile header, application icon and browser favicon. Confirm contrast and text rendering at common sizes. The Phase 4 design system may refine sizing/placement and supporting tokens but must retain the agreed professional tone and dark/light compatibility.
